# Twilio for SMS delivery, with softened Joke wording

Twilio — and every other mainstream SMS provider, via shared carrier-level CTIA rules enforced by AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile — explicitly prohibits cannabis-referencing content in the US, even non-commercial jokes with nothing for sale. A rejected A2P 10DLC campaign registration can't be resubmitted, so the risk isn't hypothetical. We chose to keep Twilio and real SMS delivery, but write every Joke with softened wording (culture/vibe references, avoiding explicit slang/keywords) rather than switching channels or accepting the compliance/ToS risk of explicit content.

## Considered Options

- **Register candidly and test vetting**: risks burning the brand/campaign registration on a non-resubmittable rejection.
- **Accept the ToS risk at hobby scale**: a real chance of account suspension if content is ever flagged.
- **Switch to email**: sidesteps carrier content policy entirely, but drops the "a text lands at 4:20" premise that's the point of the app.

## Consequences

Every Joke needs a wording pass for carrier-safety before it ships — this is an ongoing constraint on content, not a one-time setup cost.
